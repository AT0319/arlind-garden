# <img src="Benz.png" width="100" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 12px;"> Logistics & Material Flow at Mercedes-Benz Group 

**Duration:** August 2024 - September 2024  
**Location:** Untertürkheim, Germany  

During my time at Mercedes-Benz, I worked directly in the logistics department to ensure the smooth operation of internal supply chains. Gaining this hands-on operational experience was critical for understanding the physical mechanics of intralogistics, allowing me to manage the physical movement of high-value automotive components effectively.

> [!info] Proactive Qualification
> Before entering the active warehouse floor, I wanted to ensure immediate operational readiness. I independently completed my official industrial forklift certification (Staplerschein) through DEKRA just three weeks prior to my start date.

### Core Responsibilities & Operations

* **Heavy Material Handling:** Safely operated specialized industrial forklifts equipped with 2-meter extended forks to maneuver oversized and complex automotive pallets.
* **Process Execution:** Executed secure transport, targeted material retrieval, and internal logistics processes to guarantee an uninterrupted supply chain on the shop floor.
* **Systems Integration:** Managed material procurement and tracked inventory workflows using enterprise ERP/SAP systems.
* **Operational Safety:** Maintained strict compliance with Mercedes-Benz's internal safety protocols and space-management standards on a highly active warehouse floor.

# Freelance Academic Tutor

**Duration:** October 2021 - Present

* Providing tailored academic support across a wide variety of subjects for students ranging from 1st to 12th grade, as well as foundational university-level courses.
* Breaking down complex concepts into easily digestible lessons, continuously enhancing my own communication, patience, and mentoring skills.

### Automated Performance Tracking & Process Optimization
To optimize my tutoring workflow and accurately track student progress, I developed a custom tracking application within Excel using **Visual Basic for Applications (VBA)**. 

* **Process Automation:** Engineered macros to automate repetitive data entry and dynamically calculate grade improvements specifically adapted to the German grading system (1,0 to 6,0). 
* **Dynamic UI & Formatting:** Programmed a script that automatically applies professional styling, zebra-striping, and conditional formatting to highlight significant academic milestones (e.g., improvements of a full grade point).
* **Technical Implementation:** Utilized dynamic range detection (`End(xlUp)`) and interactive `InputBox` prompts to create a seamless, software-like user experience that scales automatically as new students are added.

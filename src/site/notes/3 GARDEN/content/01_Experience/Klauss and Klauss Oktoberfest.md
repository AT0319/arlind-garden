# <img src="Klauss_Weller_Festzelt_Logo_png.png" width="100" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 12px;"> High-Volume Operations at Klauss & Klauss

**Seasons:** 09/2023 - 10/2023 | 09/2024 - 10/2024 | 09/2025 - 10/2025 | *Upcoming: 09/2026 - 10/2026*

Working at the Oktoberfest is a masterclass in operations and stress management. Over multiple seasons, I have operated in an extremely high-volume, high-stress festival environment, seamlessly shifting between bartending (Zapfer), serving (Kellner), and floor management.

* **Logistics & Speed:** Coordinated service logistics and optimized workflows to handle massive crowds efficiently during peak hours.
* **Crisis Management:** Demonstrated high adaptability, resilience, and crisis management skills by diplomatically navigating complex crowd dynamics.
* **De-escalation:** Handled and de-escalated situations with highly intoxicated patrons while maintaining strict operational speed and safety.

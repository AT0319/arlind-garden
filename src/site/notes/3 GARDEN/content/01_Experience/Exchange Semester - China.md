# Global Immersion: SILC Shanghai

**Location:** SILC Business School, Shanghai, China  
**Timeline:** Summer 2026

Participating in an exchange semester in Shanghai has profoundly broadened my academic and professional trajectory. This experience goes far beyond traditional university coursework; it serves as a deep dive into one of the world's most dynamic and fast-paced technological hubs.

> [!info] Global Startup Ecosystem
> During my time in China, I have actively integrated into the local startup scene, building cross-cultural founder networks and currently competing in a nationwide startup competition with my venture, **[[3 GARDEN/content/02_Projects/HelioShade\|HelioShade]]**.

### Key Highlights & Experiences

* **Cross-Cultural Operations:** Navigating complex academic and business environments in a highly international setting, significantly improving my adaptability, resilience, and intercultural communication skills.
* **Global Pitching:** Representing HelioShade on an international stage, pitching sustainable energy infrastructure and smart systems to foreign investors and technical juries.
* **Academic Expansion:** Broadening my core industrial engineering foundation (Wirtschaftsingenieurwesen) by studying emerging market trends and global business strategies at SILC.
* **Language & Cultural Immersion:** Developing foundational proficiency in Mandarin while adapting to the operational pace and cultural nuances of daily life in Shanghai.

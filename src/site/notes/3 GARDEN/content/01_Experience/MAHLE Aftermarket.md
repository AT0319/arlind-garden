# <img src="mahle.jpg" width="100" style="vertical-align: middle; margin-right: 12px;"> Product Management & Aftermarket Operations at MAHLE

**Duration:** August 2026 - September 2026  

During my tenure in MAHLE's global aftermarket division, I worked directly with a world-leading supply partner that provides OEM-quality spare parts across the globe. This role allowed me to bridge the gap between technical automotive engineering and global supply chain logistics, shifting my perspective from academic theory to active product lifecycle management.

### Technical Portfolio & Product Strategy
My core focus centered around analyzing and supporting the technical product portfolio, which encompasses high-performance automotive components used by major international vehicle manufacturers as standard equipment. 

* **Thermal Management Systems:** Handled technical workflows for thermostats and charge air coolers (Ladeluftkühler), which are critical for modern engine efficiency and temperature regulation.
* **Engine & Valve Train Components:** Supported lifecycle management for high-precision internal components designed to rigorous OEM standards.
* **Forced Induction & Filtration:** Gained exposure to complex component assemblies, including advanced turbochargers and industrial-grade filtration systems.

### Operational Insights & Supply Chain
Working hands-on with these physical components and their digital supply workflows provided me with a comprehensive understanding of the aftermarket lifecycle. 

* **Global Logistics:** Navigated the complexities of distributing critical spare parts within a fast-paced, international automotive supply chain.
* **Technical Product Management:** Analyzed component specifications to ensure seamless transitions from OEM integration to aftermarket support and distribution.

> [!success] Industry Insight
> Gained deep operational experience at the intersection of technical product management and global automotive supply chains, reinforcing my ability to manage complex industrial portfolios.
